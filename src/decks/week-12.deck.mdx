# What this course left out

SLOP3268 --- Week 12

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Turn the thesis on itself

Eleven weeks praised leaving things out.

Now we test whether that rule holds.

---

## A suspicious pattern

We found the same move in theology, painting, music, fiction, film, architecture, design, statistics, mathematics and politics.

A course that praises restraint should ask if it just picked the examples that fit.

So today is co-taught. The two of us disagree on the record.

---

## The counter-case: more is more

Some work wins by refusing to leave anything out.

A Kehinde Wiley portrait crowds every inch. A Bollywood number packs the screen.

Maximalism means art that adds rather than cuts. It can be great.

## Bad work leaves things out too

An unfinished essay is missing things. A rushed edit is missing things.

If leaving out were enough for quality, those would count as good. They don't.

---

## The second clause

So the thesis always had a hidden second half.

Not every cut earns its keep. This course showed you cuts that worked.

It was better at that than at teaching you to spot the difference in advance.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## The real question

Leaving something out is easy. Knowing which cut earns its keep is the whole job.
