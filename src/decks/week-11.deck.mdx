## Two ways to leave something out

One you can see.

One you can't.

---

## The kind you can see

A redacted document has black bars over the words someone removed.

Redaction means blacking out text so you can't read it.

The gap is printed right there. You know exactly where it is, even if not what it said.

## The kind you can't see

An externality is a cost that lands on someone but never gets counted.

Think of carbon, care work, or noise left out of a model's sums.

Nothing looks missing. The numbers still add up. That is what makes it dangerous.

---

## The speech that argues by omission

A politician can describe only the opponent's plan, never their own.

It sounds like a full argument. The gap where their policy should be is invisible.

## The black bars are the argument

An argument that works by what it refuses to say is a redaction with the bars taken off.
