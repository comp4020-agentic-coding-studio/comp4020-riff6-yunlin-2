# Less is a verb

SLOP3268 --- Week 8

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## "Less" is a thing you do

Not a look you copy.

A design gets to "less" by taking something away.

---

## The line everyone quotes

Dieter Rams's tenth principle: "good design is as little design as
possible."

People read it as a licence for blankness. Rams's own products say
otherwise.

---

## What Rams actually did

The Braun T3 radio and the SK4 record player took things out:

- visible screws and fixings
- ornament
- controls you did not need

These were designs that used to have those parts. You can feel the choice
to remove them. That is why the objects read as clean, not because
nothing is there.

---

---

## The central distinction

A design that took something out still carries the shape of what it lost,
the way a scar carries the shape of a wound.

A design that simply never had the feature was never making an argument
at all.

---

## Most "minimalism" is the second kind

Blank work that never removed anything gets to borrow the reputation of
work that did.

It looks the same at a glance. It is not the same. One made a cut. The
other just started empty.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## The cut has to be legible

If the removal does not show, you did not subtract.

You just left the space empty and hoped it would read as thought.

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Before the seminar

Bring one thing from your own studio work.

Say whether you removed a part, or never put it there, and how a viewer
could tell.
