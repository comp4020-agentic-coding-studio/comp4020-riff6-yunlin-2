# Proof by elimination

SLOP3268 --- Week 10

## Two ways to prove

Build toward the answer, step by step.

Or rule out every other answer until one is left standing.

---

## Reductio ad absurdum

A reductio proves a claim by attacking its opposite.

Assume the opposite is true. Follow it until it breaks, until it says something plainly false. If the opposite can't be true, the claim must be.

You never built the answer. You cornered it.

## Occam's razor

Same instinct, made general.

When two accounts both fit, prefer the one with fewer assumptions. Not because plain is prettier, but because every extra assumption is one more thing that can be wrong.

Cut what you don't need to carry.

---

## The elegant proof

A proof can be correct and still clumsy: too many steps, spare parts left in.

Mathematicians call the clean one **elegant**. Fewer moves. Nothing carried that isn't load-bearing. It is the same clean cut a film editor makes.

---

## Erdős's Book

Paul Erdős imagined a book God keeps, holding the single most elegant proof of every theorem.

A joke, but a serious one. It says a whole field shares a quiet standard for which cut was the right one. Argued over. Taken seriously. Never once called taste.
