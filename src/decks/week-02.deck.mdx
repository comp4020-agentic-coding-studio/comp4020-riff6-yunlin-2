# The apophatic turn

SLOP3268 --- Week 2

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## Two ways to describe a thing

Say what it is.

Or say what it is not.

---

## The words we start with

Pseudo-Dionysius, on God:

- not good
- not wise
- not real

Not because those words are wrong. Because each one is too small for
what it is pointing at.

Every word you add stops short. Crossing the word out admits that, and
lands nearer the mark.

---

## Two words worth keeping

- **cataphatic**: describing by saying (adding words)
- **apophatic**: describing by unsaying (taking them away)

Every later week has its own version of this pair. Watch for it.

---

## Three thinkers, one move

- Pseudo-Dionysius: no word from the created world fits God
- Maimonides: run that through strict logic, and it is the only honest
  theology
- The Cloud of Unknowing: not just unsayable, but unthinkable

## "Unsayable" is not "unimportant"

Refusing to name a thing can be the most exact thing you say about it.
