# Negative space

SLOP3268 --- Week 7

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## The part you leave empty

A design is not only the marks you make.

It is also the room you refuse to fill.

---

## A building made of emptiness

John Pawson's Nový Dvůr monastery treats emptiness as a material.

- thick walls
- plain light
- almost no furniture

The monks chose him because his work already looked built for silence.
Here the emptiness is the point. You are meant to dwell in it.

## The hidden arrow

The FedEx logo hides an arrow in the gap between the "E" and the "x."

It works because almost nobody sees it. You only feel that the mark
points forward.

Here the gap is a trick. It is meant to work without being noticed at all.

---

## Not the same job

Both are "negative space," but they do different work.

- Pawson: the emptiness is the thing. Notice it. Stay in it.
- FedEx: the emptiness is a device. Never notice it.

Mixing these two up is where student negative-space projects usually go
wrong.

## Ask what the empty part is for

Before you leave a space blank, know its job.

A gap to be felt is not a gap to be lived in.

## Before the seminar

Bring one design with a meaningful gap.
